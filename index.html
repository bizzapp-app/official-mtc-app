About Mercylife Training College

Mercylife Training College is a TVETA and Nursing Council of Kenya accredited tertiary health institution owned by Mercylite Hospital.

Direct Clinical Exposure at Mercylite Hospital

Our students benefit from immediate clinical rotations at Mercylite Hospital's outpatient, surgical, casualty, pediatric, and maternity departments under direct mentorship from experienced doctors and senior nursing officers.

Our Accredited Medical Programs

Applications are open for January, May, and September intakes.

DCM-101

Diploma in Clinical Medicine & Surgery

3-Year program preparing clinical officers in diagnosis, minor surgery, and outpatient emergency management.

Requirement: KCSE C Plain (C in Biology & Chemistry)
DNS-201

Diploma in Nursing (KRCHN)

Nursing Council accredited program covering adult nursing, pediatric care, and midwifery practice.

Requirement: KCSE C Plain (C in Biology & Chemistry)
MLT-501

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology

Diagnostic lab training in hematology, microbiology, clinical biochemistry, and blood transfusion.

Requirement: KCSE C Plain
